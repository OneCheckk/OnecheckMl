PDF Forensics: "The File Time Machine"

🔬 Deep Binary PDF Inspection

PDFs are structured containers. When legitimately signed, Acrobat appends a new cross-reference table (startxref … %%EOF) without purging previous blocks. Forgers exploit this to paint over approved numbers.

  • Incremental Revision Counting: Regex tallying of startxref offsets and %%EOF markers. Files with > 1 revision indicate post-issuance tampering.
  • Timestamp Divergence: Compares /CreationDate vs /ModDate inside the document information dictionary. Any delta > 24h triggers an audit alert.
  • Blacklisted Producer Signatures: Detects telltale fingerprints of editing tools: Photoshop, Canva, GIMP, Illustrator, Smallpdf, Sejda.
  • Invisible White-Text Masking: Scans stream bodies for (1 1 1 rg) operators used to hide original values under white rectangles.

📐 Mathematical Foundation of ELA

JPEG is a lossy compression format operating on 8×8 discrete cosine transform (DCT) blocks. Each compression generation causes predictable high-frequency quantization degradation until an error equilibrium is reached.

Δ(x,y) = | I_original(x,y) - I_recompressed(x,y) | × α

When an attacker splices an uncompressed seal or a foreign signature onto a certificate:

  • Quantized Background: The authentic background re-compresses with minimal delta (already at equilibrium).
  • Foreign Element Spike: The newly pasted seal generates a 5×–10× higher error delta because its high frequencies have not yet experienced the same generational loss.
  • Connected Component Labeling: SciPy ndimage.label clusters pixels above threshold $T_{suspect}$ to emit exact bounding box coordinates $(x, y, w, h)$.

👁️ Human-Perceptual Robustness

Cryptographic hashes (MD5, SHA-256) suffer from the avalanche effect: changing a single pixel flips 50% of the bits. Perceptual hashing models human vision by capturing holistic low-frequency structure.
