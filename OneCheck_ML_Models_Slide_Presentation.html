Engine 1 / 8 🕵️ The File Time Machine

Digital PDF Revision Forensics

Uncovering document edit history, spliced revisions, and invisible text

⚠️ The Fraud Trick
Colleges take an official government letterhead from a previous year, open it in Photoshop or Canva, change the approval year and sanctioned student intake, and save it as a new PDF. Some even hide invisible white text in the background to fool keyword-matching filters.
⚙️ How the Engine Catches It
A PDF is a container holding multiple incremental revisions. Our engine parses the raw binary byte stream:
  • Counts startxref tables to detect how many times the file was modified.
  • Compares /ModDate against /CreationDate.
  • Scans Producer metadata for suspicious editing tools (Photoshop, Acrobat Distiller, Canva).
  • Extracts text objects with RGB (1, 1, 1) to flag invisible ghost text.

Engine 2 / 8 🩻 The Digital X-Ray / Stamp Scanner

Error Level Analysis (ELA) Heatmap

Detecting spliced signatures, pasted stamps, and digital image manipulation

⚠️ The Fraud Trick
A college cuts the stamp and signature of a District Magistrate from a real land document and pastes it onto a fake Building Completion or Land Title certificate. To the naked human eye, the scanned resolution looks identical.
⚙️ How the Engine Catches It
Whenever a JPEG image is saved, high frequencies compress at a known error level. If an external element is pasted into the image, that pasted region has a completely different compression generation error level.
  • We resave the image in memory at a controlled 90% JPEG quality.
  • Compute the absolute pixel-by-pixel difference delta: cv2.absdiff().
  • Scale differences to highlight forged zones: genuine areas fade to dark gray, while pasted signatures glow brightly like a neon sign.
  • Compute statistical variance: score > 0.40 triggers fraud alert.

Engine 3 / 8 📸 The Carbon-Copy / Duplicate Photo Catcher

Perceptual Image Hashing (pHash)

Crushing infrastructure photo recycling and multi-branch image re-use

⚠️ The Fraud Trick
An educational trust runs three engineering colleges across a state. They have one state-of-the-art Computer Lab and Physics Lab. They photograph it once, crop it slightly, adjust the brightness, and submit the same photos for all 3 college approvals.
⚙️ How the Engine Catches It
Traditional cryptographic hashes (like MD5 or SHA-256) change completely if even 1 pixel is altered. pHash mimics human perception:
  • Downscales photo to 32x32 grayscale.
  • Computes 2D Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) to isolate structural frequencies.
  • Generates a 64-bit fingerprint string.
  • Calculates Hamming Distance (number of bit differences) against all previously submitted photos in the central index.
  • Hamming Distance ≤ 4 indicates the identical photo despite cropping or re-encoding.

⏱️ Execution Latency: 45ms per image comparison.
Engine 4 / 8 📍 Satellite Pin Tracker

500m Geo-Fence EXIF Spatial Engine

Verifying that site inspection photos were genuinely taken on the registered campus

⚠️ The Fraud Trick
AICTE requires geocoded photos of laboratories, sports facilities, and barrier-free ramps. Colleges download stock photos of fancy campus buildings from the internet or take photos inside an off-campus commercial office or shopping complex.
⚙️ How the Engine Catches It
Every genuine camera or smartphone photo embeds EXIF GPS coordinates:
  • The engine parses EXIF Tag 0x8825 (GPSInfo) to extract latitude & longitude.
  • Pulls the college's approved master campus boundaries from AICTE records.
  • Computes spherical surface distance using the Haversine Formula:
    d = 2R × asin(√(sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ1)cos(φ2)sin²(Δλ/2)))
  • If distance > 500 meters from the campus center, it is immediately rejected.

Engine 5 / 8 👓 The Smart Clause Reader

Tesseract OCR & Regulatory Regex Parser

Pinpointing sanction capacity gaps, land survey mismatches, and clause violations

⚠️ The Fraud Trick
Colleges fill out the AICTE web form claiming an approved intake of 180 students. But deep inside the 12-page scanned affiliation order issued by the State Technical University, Page 4 quietly states: "Sanctioned intake: 120 seats only". Fatigued human evaluators rarely catch this disparity.
⚙️ How the Engine Catches It
A precision document ingestion engine:
  • Image Preprocessing: Grayscale conversion → Gaussian blur → Otsu's adaptive binarization to remove old paper yellowing and watermarks.
  • Tesseract OCR: Runs page-by-page OCR extraction.
  • Deterministic Regex Patterns: Extracts legal and regulatory numbers:
    (?:Sanctioned|Approved)\s*Intake\s*[:\-]?\s*(\d{2,4})
  • Cross-references extracted number directly with the form claim field.

Engine 6 / 8 👻 Moonlighting & Ghost Faculty Radar

RapidFuzz Ghost Faculty Reconciler

Stopping dual-salary ghost professors while guaranteeing 100% Zero-PII privacy

⚠️ The Fraud Trick

⚙️ How the Engine Catches It

  • A match threshold ≥ 90% flags simultaneous active payroll status.

Engine 7 / 8 🗓️ The Scrutiny Calendar

Dynamic Validity-at-Decision-Date Engine

Certificates valid on upload day must remain valid on Scrutiny Decision Day

⚠️ The Fraud Trick
A college submits its Fire NOC or Building Safety Certificate on January 10th. At that moment, the certificate is valid. But the AICTE Scrutiny Committee convenes on May 15th. In between, on March 31st, the NOC expired! Existing portals check validity only at the time of upload, allowing expired licenses to be approved.
⚙️ How the Engine Catches It
OneCheck decouples upload time from decision time:
  • The evaluator moves a dynamic "Scrutiny Date Anchor".
  • The engine computes:
    status = f(certificate_expiry, scrutiny_anchor_date)
  • If expiry < scrutiny date → status flips dynamically to EXPIRED.
  • If expiry < scrutiny date + 30 days → warning: RENEWAL_URGENT.

Engine 8 / 8 📐 The Blueprint Architect

Multimodal Gemini Vision API

AI-powered architectural floor plan & carpet area extraction where OCR fails

⚠️ The Fraud Trick
Architectural blueprints (naksha) contain vector lines, room walls, and symbols. Traditional OCR engines fail completely or output jumbled garbage characters. Colleges take advantage of this to falsely claim 640 sq.m of computer lab area when the architect's blueprint actually shows only 410 sq.m.
⚙️ How the Engine Catches It
Our backend selectively routes architectural blueprint pages to Google Gemini 1.5 Flash API:
  • Gemini analyzes the spatial diagram, room labels, and measurement tables.
  • Extracts structured JSON: room numbers, room tags, and exact square-meter areas.
  • System compares extracted blueprint measurements against AICTE norms and form claims.
  • Deterministic matching flags discrepancies without human measurement error.
